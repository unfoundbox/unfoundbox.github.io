Hey there fellow startup builder! I have been an early stage engineer with some product chops for around 8 years now having worked with a half dozen startups from bootstrapped/pre-seed funded to unicorns.

I grew up in Rajasthan, started in Hindi-medium school, switched into CBSE, and fell hard for C++ in class 11. The path since then has been nonlinear: Kota, telecom engineering in Bangalore, a failed year that pushed me deeper into programming, and then a long run through startups.

I got my first break at Mobisy, then joined Signzy early and worked across digital KYC, APIs, and product. After that came Artoo, Protodit, a few founder experiments, Vectorly, and then Hopin / StreamYard, where I worked on applied AI, video workflows, datasets, evaluations, SDKs, and smart clip detection.

After the layoff cycle, I took a sabbatical on purpose. I wanted to slow down, rebuild healthier habits, and think clearly about what I actually wanted to make next.

My base is Udaipur, India. I still like the digital nomad rhythm, but the center of gravity now is independent building: local AI, video tooling, agentic products, and research-backed startup ideas.

This blog is the public notebook for that journey.
