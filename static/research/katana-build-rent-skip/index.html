Build, Rent, or Skip

Mapping Cardboard's entire capability stack onto Katana's roadmap — with provider picks and monthly COGS at 500 episodes.

Prepared for Sam / Katana  ·  by Saurabh  ·  June 10, 2026  ·  Source: Cardboard changelog v0.1–v0.27, launch posts, pricing page
Workload assumptions
500 episodes/month  ·  60-min average, 2–3 speakers  ·  1.5–2.5 GB Zoom gallery source  ·  10 clip candidates → 3 published clips per episode  ·  ~30% of episodes use Pro cloud render, the rest render locally  ·  All prices are mid-2026 list-price ballparks; verify before budgeting.

The organizing principle: Cardboard built two hard things in-house (a browser render engine and an agent harness), built one medium thing (the CV reframing stack), and rents everything else. Katana should make the opposite trade in one place — own the media intelligence, rent only small bounded LLM passes — and refuse the browser-engine debt entirely.

Rent — commodity brains. One provider each, bounded calls.

Cardboard's tell here is their own token-usage meter — VLM/LLM spend is material for them because they analyze all footage open-endedly. Katana rents only narrow passes per episode.

Revenue context: 500 episodes ≈ 150–200 active shows. At 40% Pro conversion × $20/mo → $1,200–1,600 MRR against ≤$630 COGS. Margins hold even at small scale and improve with GPU batching — which is what makes the $20 price and a generous free tier sustainable weapons, not discounts.

Katana's cost shape

GPU-minutes

Own models on owned/rented GPUs. Cost per episode is fixed and falls with batching. Free tier renders locally — near-zero marginal cost, plus a privacy story.

Cardboard's cost shape

Tokens

Full-footage VLM analysis (~$0.30+ per hour of video on Flash-class pricing alone), metered agent turns, rented voice, cloud proxies — plausibly $1.50–4 per project, scaling with gigabytes uploaded, not episodes shipped. Hence the token meter in their UI and the $60 entry price.

Sequencing

Weeks 1–4
Cleanup strength picker · clip rationale pass · thumbnail template preview (soft-locked) · R2 migration if not already there. All rent-side, all under ~$0.06/episode combined, all directly answer the UX audit.
Months 1–2
Brand kits / show memory (the moat) · edit-request harness with checkpoint-style revert · chapters + description bundle behind Pro with free preview.
Month 3+
Premiere XML escape hatch for the agency tier · Zoom Marketplace ingestion · publish integrations (YouTube with chapters/description/thumbnail pre-filled).
Before any of it
The ~$120 weekend test: same 3 real Zoom episodes through Katana and Cardboard, in front of 5 users — approve-the-cut vs. prompt-the-agent. If prompting wins with podcasters, re-orient before building.
Caveats. All COGS figures are mid-2026 list-price ballparks assembled from public pricing and standard throughput assumptions — verify GPU rates, ASR pricing, and model prices against current provider docs before budgeting. Cardboard inferences are evidence-based reads of their public changelog (v0.1–v0.27), launch posts, and job listings — confirmed tells include Bedrock + Vertex AI multi-model support, Mediabunny, GCS, Sentry, prompt caching, and cloud-VLM analysis; specific model choices (e.g., Gemini for video understanding, ElevenLabs for voice) are high-confidence inferences, not confirmed facts.
