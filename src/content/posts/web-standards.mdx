## Breakthrough High-Potential Opportunities

### 1. Browser-Based Cloud Gaming
- Immediate opportunity with strong technical feasibility and clear monetization paths
- WebGPU enables previously impossible rendering and compute capabilities without native installations
- Low barrier to entry aligns with gaming accessibility trends

### 2. Neural Browser Engine
- Transformative opportunity combining emerging Web APIs (WebNN, WebGPU, WebAssembly)
- Expands browser capabilities into local intelligence and on-device processing

### 3. Interactive Live Streaming & Low-Latency Media
- Rapidly growing market seeking differentiated tools and creator monetization
- WebTransport and WebCodecs enable sub-second latency and interactive video pipelines

## Strategic Takeaway

The clearest near-term AI opportunity in this space has narrowed from broad "AI in the browser" to **local, task-specific inference on WebGPU**. The interesting wedge is not giant chatbots in tabs; it is small models that win because the data should stay local, the latency must be live, the network may be unavailable, or cloud egress breaks unit economics.
